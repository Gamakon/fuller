## 07:30–08:00Z: the GPU wedge, and a hole in the safety layer
- Run 6 stopped at generation 181: the shipped kernel timed at zero. Cause (mutation-builder): wgpu-core 0.21's wait gives up after 5 s and reports the work done; a random kernel (fit_SF_r6/kernels/g180_r62.p2.e0.wgsl) was still running, so later timings read unwritten timestamps. Fixed: fuller ebb02db / phylu 5fac6d3f — a wait at or past 4.9 s is a runaway, scored infeasible, drained before anything is read; device test passes. No earlier correct or timed kernel was read early.
- Timing r62 alone left it running on the GPU after its process died: every GPU job now runs about 3.7× slow (shipped 3.8 s a shape, two probes). **The GPU needs a reboot.**
- Cause of the hang, found independently by both agents: r62 computes an integer modulo by zero. WGSL defines x % 0 = 0, but naga 0.20 writes integer `%` and `/` to Metal raw (its writer.rs:1605 has a TODO for exactly this), and Metal leaves them undefined. About 140 of 1,000 random kernels carried one; no saved kernel did. Fixed: fuller f17ccb9 — every integer `/` and `%` whose divisor may be zero is written guarded with WGSL's exact values (u32 `% max(b,1u)`; i32 helpers handling 0, −1 and MIN); 0 of 1,548 left raw in 1,000 random kernels. The GPU confirmation (r62 guarded finishes; bisection in logs/HGP/001/r62_bisect/) waits for the reboot.
