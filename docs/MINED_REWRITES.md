## Edit classes

Each edit is the smallest pair of differing sub-trees with everything around them identical (GumTree matching, then an aligned top-down walk). Most replies rewrite the whole kernel, so a pair carries many edits of many classes; the mean gain of a class is over the pairs that show it and is confounded by the other edits in those pairs. No pair here has edits of one class only, so the last two columns give the mean gain of the pairs that do not show the class, for comparison.
